4.3.0 test build 4 - the relay hands back to your home network on its own; everything from builds 1-3 below still applies.

BACK TO THE LOCAL NETWORK (BUILD 4)
- On the relay (mobile data), PC asleep or OBS closed: come home, Wi-Fi joins, the session stays on the relay. Wake the PC / start OBS: within about 30 s the toast says it moved to the local network
- Wrong saved OBS password: it tries once, stays on the relay, and doesn't try again until Wi-Fi changes (no repeated 'reconnecting')

REMOTE ACCESS FIXES (BUILD 3)
- Saved connection with an OBS password, on the relay (mobile data): ... -> Edit Connection, rename it, save. Back on Wi-Fi it connects on the LAN without asking for the password again
- On the relay, then walk in at home while OBS on the PC has a different password than the one saved: the session stays on the relay (no 'reconnecting' loop, no drop to Home)
- Globe sheet -> Turn Off on This Device now asks first (on the relay it says the session ends)
- Delete a paired saved connection (edit dialog -> Delete): OBS Blade Remote on the PC no longer lists this device

Build 2: adds 120 Hz on Android and the Bars & Sheets setting; everything from build 1 below still applies.

BARS & SHEETS (NEW)
- Settings -> Theme -> Bars & Sheets: Glass / Clear / Solid, Glass by default
- Glass: top bars, tab bar, sheets and toasts blur what's behind them - now on Android too for sheets
- Clear: no blur, slightly see-through; Solid: fully opaque - switch and check the dashboard, a sheet (audio settings) and a toast
- The setting sticks after restarting the app
- iOS: the '...' menu and alert dialogs keep the native iOS look in every mode

ANDROID: 120 HZ (NEW)
- On a 120 Hz phone: Developer options -> Show refresh rate, scroll Home / Settings / dashboard - it should read 120 (was 60 on Nothing, Xiaomi and others)
- Battery saver may still lower it

REMOTE ACCESS (PRO, NEW)
- On the PC: install OBS Blade Remote (the app is the recommended one; the plugin works too) from obs-blade.kounex.com/remote - OBS's WebSocket server on
- In the app, connected to that OBS on your Wi-Fi: the globe in the dashboard bar -> Set Up Remote Access -> paired within a few seconds, the PC shows this device
- Turn Wi-Fi off (mobile data): the app switches to the relay on its own, a toast names the route; scenes, audio, preview and stats work as on the LAN
- Back on Wi-Fi: it moves back to the local network
- Close OBS on the PC while away: the saved card / sheet say OBS isn't running; with the app on the PC, Start OBS starts it (no Safe Mode question after a crash)
- Plugin only on the PC + OBS closed: the sheet points to the app for starting OBS
- Sheet: Rename this device, Remove another; Unpair This PC in OBS Blade Remote removes access on the phone
- Without Pro: the globe shows the pitch with Unlock Pro; the paywall lists "Control OBS From Anywhere" second

STUDIO MODE: DOUBLE TAP (NEW)
- Settings -> Studio Mode on: a new "Double Tap to Transition" switch below it, on by default
- Dashboard in Studio Mode: one tap puts a scene in Preview at once (no delay); a quick second tap on the same tile sends it to Program with OBS' transition + duration, Studio Mode stays on
- Two quick taps on different tiles: only previews; a slow second tap: only a preview
- Switch off: a quick second tap is just another preview pick
- VoiceOver / TalkBack: the scene tile offers "Transition to program" as an action
