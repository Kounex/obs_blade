OBS Blade is your stream companion. Connect to OBS over your network and run your whole show from your phone or tablet - no need to jump back to your PC mid-stream.

Built for phone and tablet alike - and new in 4.1: vertical canvases for OBS 32.1, plus an activity feed and chat read out loud with Pro.

EVERYTHING YOU NEED - FREE, FOREVER

- Start and stop your stream, recording, replay buffer and virtual camera
- Switch scenes, with studio mode and transitions
- Show and hide sources in any scene
- Mix audio with live volume meters, or mute in one tap
- Watch a live scene preview
- Vertical and extra canvases (OBS 32.1+): browse their scenes and sources, and run Aitum Vertical's own stream, recording and replay buffer
- Switch profiles and scene collections, trigger hotkeys
- Keep an eye on stream health: FPS, CPU, bitrate, dropped frames
- Look back on every past stream and recording in your statistics
- Twitch, YouTube, Kick and Owncast chat in its own tab - or next to your controls while you're live
- Arrange the dashboard your way

OBS BLADE PRO

Chat is where your stream lives, so Pro makes it native - and makes sure no supporter gets missed:

- Every chat, one place: native Twitch, Kick and YouTube chat, on their own or merged into one live timeline where replies land on the right platform. Every channel you mod or follow, saved combos and who's live at a glance - plus 7TV, BTTV and FFZ emotes, an emote picker, autocomplete, role badges, highlights, mute words and chat search
- Never miss a supporter: an activity feed for your own channels - follows, subs, raids, cheers, Super Chats and KICKs in one list, with a to-thank queue for your shout-outs
- Hear your chat: read out loud while you play, in each message's own language if you like
- Moderate from your pocket: delete, timeout and ban on every platform, plus AutoMod, unban requests and chat modes on Twitch
- Your own themes: design custom color themes for the whole app

Pro comes monthly, yearly or as a one-time lifetime unlock. Cancel anytime in your App Store account settings.
