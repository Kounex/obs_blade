OBS Blade 4.0 is a complete redesign, built for phone and tablet.

- A new look with smoother, faster controls
- Media controls, hotkeys, replay buffer and virtual camera
- Chat gets its own tab: Twitch, YouTube, Kick and Owncast
- A more reliable connection to OBS
- New: OBS Blade Pro with native and combined chat, moderation tools, 7TV, BTTV and FFZ emotes, and custom themes
