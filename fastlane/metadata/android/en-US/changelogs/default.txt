4.1 brings two new Pro features and OBS 32.1 canvas support:

- Activity feed: follows, subs, raids, cheers, Super Chats and KICKs on your channels in one list, with a to-thank queue
- Text-to-speech: hear your chat read out loud, with a voice per language
- Canvases: view a vertical canvas and control Aitum Vertical's stream and recording
- Wake Lock (screen stays on) now covers the whole app - find it in Settings
- Changes made in OBS show up right away
