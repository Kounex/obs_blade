DISCLAIMER: This app is not in any way affiliated with OBS (Open Broadcaster Software). It's an unofficial, open source app to control OBS (https://github.com/Kounex/obs_blade)

OBS Blade is your stream companion. Connect to OBS over your network and run your whole show from your phone or tablet - no need to jump back to your PC mid-stream.

OBS Blade 4.0 is a full redesign: faster, cleaner and built for phone and tablet alike.

EVERYTHING YOU NEED - FREE, FOREVER

- Start and stop your stream, recording, replay buffer and virtual camera
- Switch scenes, with studio mode and transitions
- Show and hide sources in any scene
- Mix audio with live volume meters, or mute in one tap
- Watch a live scene preview
- Switch profiles and scene collections, trigger hotkeys
- Keep an eye on stream health: FPS, CPU, bitrate, dropped frames
- Look back on every past stream and recording in your statistics
- Twitch, YouTube, Kick and Owncast chat in its own tab - or next to your controls while you're live
- Arrange the dashboard your way

OBS BLADE PRO

Chat is where your stream lives, so Pro makes it native:

- Native chat for Twitch, Kick and YouTube - read and write without a browser in sight
- Combined chat: all your chats in one live timeline, and replies land on the right platform
- Every channel you mod or follow, saved combos and who's live at a glance
- A full moderation toolkit: delete, timeout and ban on every platform, plus AutoMod, unban requests and chat modes on Twitch
- 7TV, BTTV and FFZ emotes, an emote picker, autocomplete and role badges
- Highlights, mute words, chat search - and custom themes for the whole app

Pro comes monthly, yearly or as a one-time lifetime unlock. Cancel anytime in your store account.
